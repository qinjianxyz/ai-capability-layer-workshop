# Tooling Options

Current as of May 21, 2026. Verify current plan limits and institutional controls before adoption.

- Canvas: no setup; best for workflow design and governance.
- Web chat: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and similar tools; best for drafting, critique, synthesis, and teaching materials with safe context.
- Builder agent: Codex, Claude Code, Copilot, and similar tools; best for files, scripts, tests, workflow kits, and reusable skills.
- Local models: Ollama, LM Studio, and similar options; useful for private or offline experimentation with quality and hardware tradeoffs.
- Orchestration: OpenClaw, Hermes-style sessions, GBrain, GStack, MCP servers, and custom skills; useful when workflows span memory, sessions, permissions, tools, and handoffs.
